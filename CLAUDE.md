# Slowlife Family Farm website

Plain static site (no build step) for www.slowlifefamilyfarm.com.
Owner: Carlo Montesanti, with Jessie. Audience: American-English-speaking travelers. Goal: send people to book.

## Files
- `index.html` — homepage (hero film, the day in seven movements, the game, films, reviews, price, gardens, story, visit, Instagram, FAQ, contact, booking)
- `the-old-table.html` — Back to the Old Table
- `terms.html` — terms and agreements (includes photo/video consent)
- `assets/css/site.css`, `assets/js/site.js`, `assets/img/`, `assets/video/`

## Standing rules
1. "Slowlife" is always one word.
2. American English. Short, warm, plain sentences.
3. Not a technical cooking class: "a food journey into Sicilian tradition". Not everything is cooked on the fire.
4. The ancestral table game is the heart of the day. Never explain its rules; keep it a surprise. Frame it as sharing, not wine.
5. Location: "near Canicattini Bagni" plus drive times only. The exact meeting point is shared after booking.
6. No BBC / Netflix claims. No tours or hikes. No product promotion. No newsletter.
7. Videos matter: show them big, never as small thumbnails.
8. Price €150 per adult, €75 per child. 9:30 AM to 3:30/4:00 PM. Up to 25 guests.
9. Use only the farm's own photos and videos.
