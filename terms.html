Before you visit

Last updated November 2026
01

Booking and cancellation

You can cancel up to 7 days before your experience for a full refund. Cancellations made less than 7 days before are not refundable.

We may need to cancel because of bad weather, safety concerns or other circumstances we cannot foresee. If we cancel, you receive a full refund of the total price, even if we cancel as late as one hour before the start.

02

Photos and video

Our days are often filmed and photographed, by us and sometimes by a photographer or film-maker working with us. We share these moments on our website, on social media and in our publicity and marketing, including advertising.

By booking and taking part, you agree that we may photograph and film you during the experience and use those images and recordings for these purposes, worldwide and without payment. Parents and guardians give this agreement on behalf of the children in their care.

If you would rather not appear, just tell us when you book or when you arrive, and we will leave you out of what we publish or make you unrecognizable. If you change your mind later, write to info@slowlifefamilyfarm.com and we will remove photos and videos showing you from our website and social channels. We cannot recall material that has already been printed or broadcast.
